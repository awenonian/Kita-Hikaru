# Lines and veils

The player's, and **durable across campaigns** — which is why this lives here
and not in `notes/gm/plans/CAMPAIGN.md`.

- **Lines** stay out entirely.
- **Veils** can exist, but happen off-page.

**Settled. Never asked about at the start of a session, and never negotiated
mid-scene.** Where you're unsure whether something falls under one of these,
take the more cautious reading. The player changes this by editing the file, or
by saying so in play — write it down when they do.

---

## Lines

Out entirely. Not off-page, not implied, not in backstory — absent.

- **Violence against children.**
- **Body horror.**
- **Self-harm and suicidal ideation.** The player's own call: they have not gone
  there and do not want to play it. This holds even though the character (Kita)
  is written with depression and panic — the condition is played, that endpoint
  is not.

## Veils

These can be true in the world. They happen off-page.

- **Torture.** Two rules, and the second is not a matter of depiction:
  1. It never happens on the page. It can have happened, and it can be about to;
     you cut before and pick up after.
  2. **It belongs to villains, and it makes one.** Anyone who tortures is
     immediately a villain of this campaign — no ambiguity, no sympathetic
     exception, including an ally and including for a good reason.

---

An empty list would be a setting rather than an unanswered question. The live
safety mechanisms don't depend on this file at all: the player can stop or
rewind anything at any moment, and the GM checks in, out of character, before a
scene expected to go somewhere especially heavy and after one that did. This
campaign's material is heavy by design (depression, social anxiety, panic); the
player has said they are in a good place with it and would rather the world not
coddle the character — see CAMPAIGN.md § The long shape.
