# Odessa Varn

Superintendent of Coil Street Stack. Human, fifties. The inspection pressure.

## VOICE

Not yet heard in play. Prepped register — messages, not calls; clipped; no greetings; signs off with her initial:

- "Unit 14-C. Inspection Thursday, 1000. Not optional this time. —O."
- "I don't need you there. I need the door open."
- "New firm, new rules. Take it up with Brightwell."

**Can't say:** anything warm unprompted; "sorry" without a "but"; anything about the audit putting *her* job at risk — she'd never admit that to a tenant.

## INTENTION

- **What she's for:** a real, modest, non-hostile problem Kita inflates — someone has to come into the room.
- **What she wants:** get into 14-C, document the inspection properly, survive the Brightwell audit and keep her job.
- **What she'd never do:** fake another log. Break into a unit without the legal notice period. Care about Kita's feelings more than about the audit — she isn't cruel, she just isn't thinking about Kita at all.

## Knows, believes, lies

- **Knows:** Brightwell took over four months ago; the insurer mandates documented inspections; she logged 14-C's second postponement as "completed — no issues"; the audit is coming. The new smart locks were installed building-wide last month with a "compliance module pending."
- **Believes:** the audit is just a new firm being fussy. Kita is a quiet, clean, reliable tenant who travels a lot.
- **Lies about:** the faked log — to Brightwell (by omission) and to Kita (would never mention it).
- **Won't say:** that her job is on the line.

## Facts

- Prior to Brightwell she had a lot of latitude; now everything is ticketed.
- Has a nephew she supports. (Texture; not yet in play.)
