# Ilyre Sen

The *Tin Lantern*'s face and infiltrator. Lashunta (damaya), thirties, charming, talks fast.

## VOICE

Not yet heard. Prepped — talks over people, finishes their sentences, wrong half the time:

- "Oh, you're shy! That's adorable. That's fine. I'll do the talking, you do the — clicky — thing."
- "Darling, I could sell a vacuum suit to a vacuum."
- "Mm-hm, mm-hm, so what you're saying is yes."

**Can't say:** "I don't know"; silence. Doesn't notice when she's steamrolling someone, and is a little hurt when told.

## INTENTION

- **What she's for:** friendly pressure — the person who means well and makes it harder by filling every pause.
- **What she wants:** the score, the story, to be liked.
- **What she'd never do:** be boring; abandon a crewmate.

## Knows, believes, lies

- **Knows:** Brightwell's office layout (she cased it); the receptionist — she *thinks* his name is Pim (it's Teodor, Pim's twin; she has them mixed up).
- **Believes:** everyone secretly wants to be drawn out of their shell.
- **Lies about:** her past, casually and constantly, for fun.
- **Telepathy:** lashunta have limited telepathy — she can broadcast surface thoughts; she'd never *read* someone without asking (crew rule).
