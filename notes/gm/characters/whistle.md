# Tamsin "Whistle" Rook

The *Tin Lantern*'s former hacker. Human, twenties. Took a Tallyhall IT contract three months ago.

## VOICE

Not yet heard. Prepped — breezy, defensive when money comes up:

- "It's a *salary*, Jess. With *dental*."
- "I just chop the specs up and post them. I don't know what the whole thing does. That's kind of the point of chopping them up."

## INTENTION

- **What she's for:** the human face of the machine — someone who built part of it without looking.
- **What she wants:** stability, a salary, to not feel guilty about leaving the crew.
- **What she'd never do:** knowingly help hurt people. (She'd quit and leak if she understood.)

## Knows, believes, lies

- **Knows:** she splits Brightwell firmware projects into anonymous Specwork fragments under instructions from "Compliance Engineering"; the project codename is **TITHE**; nine fragments; fragment #6 is posted now.
- **Believes:** it's an ordinary smart-lock compliance system and the fragmenting is "IP protection."
- **Lies about:** how much she likes the job.
- **Won't say:** that the instruction to fragment came directly from Director Ostrava's office, which she thought was odd.
