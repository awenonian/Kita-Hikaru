# Nib

Ysoki parts dealer, stall in the Sump under Spindle Cross. Knows Miku better than he knows Kita.

## VOICE

Not yet heard in play. Prepped — talks *to Miku*, fast, cheeks full of opinions:

- "No. No no no. Who told you a Kovac servo? You're building something that *turns*, not something that *lifts*. Take this one. Same price. Don't argue."
- "Yes or no, little bird. Blink it for me."
- "Your boss writes a good list. Wrong, but good."

**Can't say:** a straight compliment to the builder's face; a price higher than the one on the list; "I'm getting old."

## INTENTION

- **What he's for:** the warm-ish edge of Kita's world; the leak through which she gets *found*.
- **What he wants:** respect for craft. Quietly, an apprentice or partner — his eyes are going for fine work. Half-hopes the builder will turn up in person one day.
- **What he'd never do:** upsell; sell bad parts; give up a customer to someone he thinks means them harm.

## Knows, believes, lies

- **Knows:** Miku comes from the spinward lifts, roughly on a schedule (every 6–8 days, late cycle). He's shown stall-cam footage of Miku's stabilised flight to customers, including a vesk engineer (Korrask) who asked who did the work.
- **Believes:** the builder is "some genius kid" — he doesn't know her name, gender, or where she lives.
- **Lies about:** nothing important. Would deny he's been showing the footage if he thought it embarrassed the builder.
- **Won't say:** that he'd like an apprentice.

## Facts

- Discounts his substitutions to the price of the original request. Never upsells.
- Old for a ysoki.

## In play

- S1 day 0: no BRW-ACX in stock (oracle). Says Brightwell's fitters reclaimed every defective unit and counted them out of the bins — he asked.
