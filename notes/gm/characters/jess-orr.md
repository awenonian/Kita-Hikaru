# Jessamy "Jess" Orr

Captain of the *Tin Lantern*, a battered light freighter berthed in Dock 9 of the Arms. Human, forties.

## VOICE

In play (S1 day 0, recorded on Korrask's chip):

- "This is Jess Orr, captain of the *Tin Lantern*. If you're hearing this, Korrask found your drone, and I'm sorry about whatever she did."
- "I don't need you to like us. I need someone who can get into a corporate system and out again without anyone noticing."
- "Forty credits to you. Twenty up front if you'll meet us. Your call."
- "I need a yes or no in two days."

Originally prepped — flat, practical, asks one question at a time and waits:

- "I don't need you to like us. I need you to get us in a door."
- "Can you do it or not. 'Maybe' is a no."
- "We pay on delivery. Half up front if you show your face. Your call."

**Can't say:** reassurance she doesn't mean; "it'll be fine"; anything sentimental about the ship (she'd deflect to the loan).

## INTENTION

- **What she's for:** the door off Absalom; an employer who is fair and not soft.
- **What she wants:** keep the ship. The *Tin Lantern*'s note is held by the **First Vault of Spindle Cross** (Abadaran). Venn's job pays down a big chunk of it.
- **This week, unopposed:** find a hacker before Venn's window closes (he wants the records within ~10 days); if she can't find Miku's builder, she'll hire a worse freelancer named **Gaspar Ilk** who drinks.
- **What she'd never do:** leave a crewmate in custody; take a job hurting civilians; lie to a crewmate about pay.

## Knows, believes, lies

- **Knows:** the job (pull Brightwell lease-processing records from a Brightwell office server in Spindle Cross); Venn is the client; their old hacker Whistle quit for a Tallyhall IT contract.
- **Believes:** it's ordinary crooked lending. Doesn't know about Mammon.
- **Lies about:** how desperate they are for money.
- **Won't say:** that the ship gets repossessed if they miss two more payments.

## Facts

- Ex-Stewards trainee, washed out. Doesn't talk about why.
