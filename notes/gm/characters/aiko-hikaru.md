# Aiko Hikaru

Kita's mother. Lives on Absalom Station, another ring, a tram ride away.

## VOICE

Not yet heard. Prepped — long messages, edits herself, ends with questions she then answers:

- "Hi sweetheart. Just checking in. You don't have to reply. I know you're busy. Love, Mom."
- "Your father says hello. He'd say it himself but you know how he is."

**Can't say:** "we're moving." (She's drafted it many times.)

## INTENTION

- **What she wants:** to know Kita's okay; to tell her about the move without it being one more unanswered message.
- **What she'd never do:** show up unannounced (a friend told her "give her space," and she's holding to it).

## Facts

- Messaged regularly until Kita's non-response became habit; has mostly stopped.
- **Unrevealed:** Tomas's employer is relocating his team to **Aballon**; they've accepted. Leaving in ~10 weeks. Haven't told Kita.
