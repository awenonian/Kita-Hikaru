# Cadmus Vell

Brightwell Residential "compliance officer." Field arm of the Tally. Human, forties, tidy, a little too calm.

## VOICE

Not yet heard. Prepped — soft, procedural, faint smile:

- "I'm just here to document."
- "Refusal of entry has been noted. Thank you for your time."

## INTENTION

- **What he's for:** the face of the threat at the door; combat if it comes to it. Accompanied by two **Brightwell security** contractors and an **imp** familiar that rides inside his briefcase (the "hands his hands are steady in a way hands shouldn't be" telegraph — the briefcase sometimes moves).
- **What he wants:** flag units, document breaches, collect.
- **What he'd never do:** enter a unit without the contract's notice clause satisfied (the contract binds him too).
