# Kita Hikaru

The player character. Mechanic, drone-first, agoraphobic coder on Absalom Station.

## VOICE

No lines in play yet. Kita is the player's to voice — never put words in her mouth. From session zero, how she *operates*:

- Mic on Miku is **habitually muted**, in case she makes a sound someone notices.
- Text over speech, always. Short, careful, pre-drafted.
- Miku's LED screen exists mostly to say yes/no to Nib.

## INTENTION

- **What she's for:** the protagonist. Her arc is the player's to find.
- **Cares about:**
  - **Miku** — proud of having *made* her; thinks of her as a tool, not a friend. (Leaning on Miku emotionally happened in the old game as a *development*. Let it be earned, not assumed.)
  - **Niche internet cultures** — many opinions, never posts. Commissioned a vtuber model hoping to stream behind a mask; never did. Player: model can be background or live — "a useful halfway between voice-only and turning her actual camera on… maybe the crew will need a mascot."
  - **Doing work correctly.** Better than most contractors, thinks she's average, always thinks she should have pushed harder. Never hears feedback.
- **Working toward:**
  - *Surface:* stay afloat, pay rent. Not happy; doesn't think about changing it; may not realise it can change.
  - *Underneath, reflexively not engaged with:* wants friends, a few people to share life with. Seeks sideways routes — be cool enough that people seek *her*. Contributes to open source under the excuse that it's good, not that she wants to be seen.
  - **Method:** cool in ways that are *self-defeatingly hard to notice* — e.g. the air-current algorithm that keeps Miku's camera perfectly still like a bird's head. Not bedazzled; that's too visible to dare.
- **What actually moves her:** being noticed for the *subtle* thing without having to present herself; threats to Miku; threats to the apartment / the rent; a non-verbal route to doing the right thing; other people's *tone*.
- **What she'd never do (as she starts):** call voice-first; ask for help; file a complaint or maintenance ticket; make herself the centre of attention on purpose; leave Miku behind.

## Personality, tested (session zero probe — NOT canon)

Grocery kiosk, ysoki clerk mid-argument, undercharges her 14 cr (her rationed coffee); fixing it means speaking through Miku. She reached for unmute and froze, finger hovering, thoughts locking up, hoping the clerk would notice on his own. Miku hovered motionless after being waved off. If he got angry → flee, then spiral about having stolen (at a scanner she'd stop, panic, put the coffee back). If mild → she'd hold out the credstick again and hope the hint landed.

**Read:** honesty is real but routes around speech. Under hostility: flight, then self-punishment. Hopes others solve the social problem for her. The player enjoys the *interior* of the choice; the variable that matters is **how the other person reacts**. Build scenes where tone is the variable and where there's a hard verbal path beside a costly non-verbal one.

## The old game (NON-canon — tone reference only)

The player ran Kita once before, in SF1e without rules skills. None of it happened in this campaign. It shows the kind of character she is:
- A crew noticed her drone and hired her. On the intro job she missed a skill check or two — the job was fine — and she cried herself to sleep over her "failures."
- When they accepted her anyway, moving onto their ship took enormous effort: they moved her stuff while she waited somewhere quiet and uncrowded, then left the ship so she could sneak aboard unseen. Even that was nearly too much.
- Small, young woman. Debilitating social anxiety, depression, prone to panic attacks. Years of not leaving her apartment.
