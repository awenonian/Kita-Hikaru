# Director Sabine Ostrava

Director of Residential Assets, Tallyhall Financial. Leader of **the Tally**, a cell of Mammon worshippers. Human, sixties.

## VOICE

Not yet heard. Prepped — unfailingly polite, financial vocabulary for everything:

- "Everyone pays. The only question is the instrument."
- "I don't take anything that wasn't offered. I simply read the terms more carefully than they did."
- "Please, sit. You've come a long way, and you'll want to be comfortable for the figures."

**Can't say:** a lie in writing (Mammon's devotees honour the letter of a contract — she lies freely in speech, never on paper); a raised voice.

## INTENTION

- **What she's for:** the antagonist at the top of the first thread. Literal devil-worship in a good suit.
- **What she wants:** souls as collateral — "assets, tangible and intangible, in this world and any other" — accumulated through manufactured lease defaults; promotion within Mammon's earthly hierarchy (the Tally reports to a larger network off-station).
- **This week, unopposed:** finish TITHE fragments #6–#9 and deploy the compliance module to all five Spindle Cross properties (~3 weeks). Push Brightwell's inspection audit so every non-compliant unit is flagged.
- **What she'd never do:** break a written contract; act where a contract doesn't cover her; get her own hands dirty.

## Knows, believes, lies

- **Knows:** everything about TITHE. Not Kita's name — 14-C is a row in a spreadsheet.
- **Believes:** she is entirely within her rights. Mostly she is, which is the horror.
