Freelance hacker; Jess's fallback. Lost the Brightwell job to Miku at the meet.
Human, wiry, thirties, cheap deck.

## VOICE

Heard at the meet (S1 day 1) only as dismissiveness toward "a flying camera" —
no verbatim lines recorded. Prepped:

- "A flying camera. You're paying a flying camera."
- "Fine. Fine! When it crashes into a wall, you've got my code."
- "Everybody sells everything, Jess. I'm just honest about it."

**Can't say:** "I was wrong"; anything admitting he needs the money.

## INTENTION

- **What he's for:** the leak. A rival made by Kita's success, not by malice.
- **What he wants:** out from under his debt to **Dunmore Kask** (half-orc Free
  Captains fence, the Low Tide bar, the Arms). Also, to be proven right about the drone.
- **What he'd never do:** go to the cops. Hurt anyone himself.

## Knows, believes, lies

- **Knows:** target is Brightwell, the field office in Spindle Cross, run "in a
  few nights." Not the exact night unless he finds out.
- **Believes:** the drone is a gimmick and the job will go wrong.
- **Lies about:** selling the job.
