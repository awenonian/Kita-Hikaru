# Hollis Venn

Abadaran contract-priest at the First Vault of Spindle Cross. The crew's client. Dwarf, older, meticulous.

## VOICE

Not yet heard. Prepped — measured, quotes clauses, never raises his voice:

- "A contract is a promise with its working shown. These do not show their working."
- "I don't need proof of malice. I need proof of fraud. Abadar can do the rest."

## INTENTION

- **What he's for:** the lever. If infernal clauses are proven, Abadar's courts can void them as fraud — which saves the tenants.
- **What he wants:** Brightwell's lease-processing records (renewal addenda + default filings) to bring a case.
- **What he'd never do:** break the law himself. Hiring freelancers who might is a grey area he has prayed about.

## Knows, believes, lies

- **Knows:** default filings in Brightwell's five Spindle Cross properties are up ~900% in four months; all cite "inspection non-compliance"; eleven households evicted so far.
- **Believes:** ordinary predatory lending. Has not read the full addendum (Brightwell won't produce it).
- **Lies about:** nothing. Omits that he's hiring people to break into a server.
