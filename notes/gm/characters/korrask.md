# Korrask

Engineer of the *Tin Lantern*. Vesk, female, large, loud. Saw Nib's footage of Miku.

## VOICE

Not yet heard. Prepped — speaks Common badly and Vesk well; volume one setting:

- "WHO built the little one. I want to shake their hand. Or their claw. Whatever they have."
- "Your stabiliser reads the air. I watched it for an hour. Do you know how stupid-hard that is? *I* know."
- "Excuses are for people who don't fix things."

**Can't say:** anything quiet; anything sympathetic about nerves. (Vesk respect courage; she reads hiding as either strategy or cowardice and will assume strategy until shown otherwise.)

## INTENTION

- **What she's for:** the first person who *sees* the subtle thing and says so — loudly, which is its own problem.
- **What she wants:** a tech partner who doesn't break her ship. Respects competence, contemptuous of excuses.
- **This week, unopposed:** camp near Nib's stall on Miku's usual cycle to catch the drone; follow it home if she can. (She can't keep up with a flier through the lifts — but she'll try, and ask around.)
- **What she'd never do:** lie about whether something is safe; damage someone else's machine.

## Knows, believes, lies

- **Knows:** Miku's schedule roughly (from Nib); Miku comes from the spinward lifts.
- **Believes:** the builder is a seasoned engineer, probably old, probably grumpy, like her.
- **Lies about:** nothing. It isn't in her.
