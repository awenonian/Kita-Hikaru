# Session 1 — plan

Prep the world, not the plot. Everything below is what's *true* and what each
party does if unopposed. None of it is a sequence.

## Situation on day 0

- Kita has 1 credit. Rent due day 9.
- **Rent is 30 cr/month** (scaled to Player Core cost of living: Comfortable = 40 cr/month all-in). Due day 9. She has 1 cr because a client cancelled a sizeable contract last week without paying (Specwork disputes take 30 days). TITHE fragment #6 pays **60 cr** — suspiciously generous for a module that size. Ordinary Specwork jobs pay 2–8 cr and take a day or two each (Earn Income, Computers via Programmer — look up the Earn Income table before resolving).
- Miku is due a Sump run (groceries + parts) — her usual cycle is today or tomorrow, late cycle.
- Korrask has been waiting at Nib's stall two cycles running.

## Parties this week, unopposed

- **The Tally:** fragment #6 goes to another contractor on day 2 if Kita doesn't take it; #7 posts day 3. Odessa's notice day 1 → inspection day 6, Cadmus Vell attends.
- **Odessa:** sends the notice; on day 4 sends "Confirming Thursday 1000"; on day 6 arrives with Vell at 1000 sharp.
- **The crew:** Korrask watches Nib's; on contact she's loud and delighted. Jess wants a yes/no within 2 days. Ilyre wants to meet "properly." The Brightwell run is planned for day 5 night (before the inspection — coincidence the player may notice).
- **Lolo:** messages on day 2.
- **Nib:** on Miku's visit, rewrites the list as usual; this time adds "someone's been asking about you, little bird."

## Possible openings (commit to none)

1. **Morning in 14-C.** Rent reminder ping. The Specwork contract sitting in the queue. Miku charging. The damp corner. (Quiet, interior; lets the player set her rhythm.)
2. **Miku at Nib's stall**, late cycle, and a very large vesk is leaning on the counter, who straightens up when the drone arrives.
3. **The inspection notice arrives** while she's reading the contract spec — "Not optional this time. —O."

Lean toward 1, folding 3 in early, so the player starts in her own space and the world arrives.
