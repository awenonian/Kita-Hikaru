## House rules (decided in session zero)

- **Drone control range ignored.** Player's choice. Kita can control Miku at any distance (RAW is 150 ft). The friction instead comes from what Miku *can't* do: it's a robot companion with weaker skills, can't do what a person could, and sometimes the job needs Kita physically (e.g. she has to go put out the fire herself). Make that friction real when it comes up; don't make Miku a skeleton key.
- **Anxiety has no mechanics.** The player roleplays it directly; no frightened condition or anxiety stat. GM MAY apply an ad hoc circumstance penalty to a check when her state would plausibly impair it ("you're shaking"); the player explicitly OKs that.
