# Positions

Something a character committed to, out loud or by acting: what they said, to
whom, and what it cost them.

A man who looks at stolen property in a cart and decides to say nothing has
taken a position, and it is the most important thing that happened in that
scene.

**After every scene, ask: did anyone here take a position?**

Positions taken don't survive unless they're written down. A remembered one is
already lost.

Format, loosely:

> **Who** — what they committed to. To whom. What it cost. *(session/scene)*

---

> **Kita** — accepted the access-controller firmware contract (60 cr) on Specwork, knowing the spec was missing its interfaces, and decided to pull them off her own door lock rather than ask the client. Cost: nothing yet; she's now committed to writing it within 4 days. *(S1, day 0)*

> **Kita** — her standing rule: if a Specwork contract is obviously for one of the Asmodean corps, she turns it down. Stated to herself, S1 day 1. *(Not yet tested.)*
>
> **Kita** — messaged Jess Orr: "What corp?" — her first direct contact with the crew. Cost: an hour of not-sending. *(S1 day 1)*

> **Kita** — agreed (via `yes` on Miku's screen) to join the *Tin Lantern* crew's job: breach the Brightwell field office and pull the lease-processing records for their client. Took a 20 cr advance. *(S1, day 1, the meet)*
>
> **Jess Orr** — committed, in front of her crew, to running the records job for "one of the good ones," and chose Kita/Miku over Ilk on the strength of the work. *(S1, day 1)*
